## 🌸 1. A Life of Family and Generations

Elizabeth Rebholz was the wife of Edward Rebholz and
the mother of eight children.

Their family included Cecelia “Ceil” Wacker, the
youngest of the eight children.

Through her children and grandchildren, Elizabeth
became part of a growing family whose history
continues across generations.

This tribute honors her place in that family while
leaving room for personal memories and historical
details still to be discovered.

## 🕯️ 7. A Memorial Tribute

### 🌹 The Roots of a Family

Elizabeth Rebholz's place in family history begins
with a simple and remarkable fact: she was the
mother of eight children.

Together with Edward, she helped establish a family
whose branches would extend across generations.

Among those children was Cecelia, who would become
the mother of Jason, Aaron, Nathan, and Nicole Wacker.

Each generation carries its own experiences,
discoveries, joys, and sorrows. Yet every generation
is connected to the people who came before it.

Elizabeth's story deserves to be remembered not
only through names and dates, but through the voices
of her children and grandchildren.

May the stories still waiting to be told bring
her life into clearer focus.
