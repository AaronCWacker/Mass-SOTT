# ⛪ The Catholic Mass — Childhood Edition (1970s–1980s)

## 🕊️ I. Introductory Rites

1. 🔔 Entrance Procession
   - Entrance hymn
   - Priest and altar servers enter

2. ✝️ Sign of the Cross
   - In the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit
   - Amen

3. 🤝 Greeting
   - The Lord be with you
   - And also with you

4. ❤️ Penitential Rite
   - I confess to almighty God
   - Confession of sins
   - Asking forgiveness

5. 🙏 Kyrie
   - Lord, have mercy
   - Christ, have mercy
   - Lord, have mercy

6. 🌟 Gloria
   - Glory to God in the highest
   - Peace to his people on earth
   - Praise of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit

7. 📜 Opening Prayer
   - Let us pray
   - Amen

## 📖 II. Liturgy of the Word

8. 📚 First Reading
   - Usually Old Testament
   - This is the word of the Lord
   - Thanks be to God

9. 🎵 Responsorial Psalm
   - Psalm and congregational response

10. 📖 Second Reading
    - Letters of the apostles
    - Thanks be to God

11. 🎶 Gospel Acclamation
    - Alleluia, Alleluia

12. ✨ Gospel
    - Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John
    - Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ

13. 🎙️ Homily
    - Scripture explained
    - Lessons for daily life

## ✝️ III. Profession of Faith — Nicene Creed

14. 🌍 God the Father
    - We believe in one God
    - Father Almighty
    - Maker of heaven and earth
    - All that is seen and unseen

15. ☀️ Jesus Christ
    - Only Son of God
    - Eternally begotten of the Father
    - God from God
    - Light from Light
    - True God from true God
    - One in being with the Father

16. 🕊️ Incarnation
    - By the power of the Holy Spirit
    - Born of the Virgin Mary
    - Became man

17. 🩸 Crucifixion
    - For our sake he was crucified
    - Under Pontius Pilate
    - He suffered, died, and was buried

18. 🌅 Resurrection
    - On the third day he rose again
    - In fulfillment of the Scriptures
    - Ascended into heaven
    - Seated at the right hand of the Father

19. 👑 Second Coming
    - He will come again in glory
    - Judge the living and the dead
    - His kingdom will have no end

20. 🔥 Holy Spirit
    - Lord and giver of life
    - Proceeds from Father and Son
    - Spoken through the prophets

21. ⛪ Church
    - One, holy, catholic, apostolic Church
    - One baptism for forgiveness of sins

22. ♾️ Eternal Life
    - Resurrection of the dead
    - Life of the world to come
    - Amen

## 🙏 IV. Prayer of the Faithful

23. 🌎 Universal Prayer
    - For the Church
    - For world leaders and peace
    - For the sick and suffering
    - For families and communities
    - For the departed
    - Lord, hear our prayer

## 🍞 V. Liturgy of the Eucharist

24. 🍇 Preparation of Gifts
    - Bread and wine brought to altar
    - Collection and offertory hymn

25. 🥖 Blessing of Bread
    - Blessed are you, Lord God of all creation
    - Fruit of the earth
    - Work of human hands

26. 🍷 Blessing of Wine
    - Fruit of the vine
    - Work of human hands
    - Spiritual drink

27. 💧 Mixing Water and Wine
    - Humanity and divinity of Christ

28. 🫧 Washing of Hands
    - Purification from sin

29. 🙌 Invitation to Prayer
    - Pray, brethren
    - May the Lord accept the sacrifice
      at your hands
    - Praise and glory of his name
    - Good of all his Church

30. 🕯️ Prayer over the Gifts
    - Offerings entrusted to God
    - Amen

## 🔔 VI. Eucharistic Prayer — Great Thanksgiving

31. 🤝 Preface Dialogue
    - The Lord be with you
    - And also with you
    - Lift up your hearts
    - We lift them up to the Lord
    - Let us give thanks to the Lord our God
    - It is right to give him thanks and praise

32. 🌟 Preface
    - Thanksgiving to the Father
    - Salvation through Christ
    - Joining angels and saints

33. 👼 Sanctus
    - Holy, holy, holy Lord
    - God of power and might
    - Heaven and earth filled with glory
    - Hosanna in the highest

34. 🕊️ Epiclesis
    - Invocation of the Holy Spirit
    - Consecration of bread and wine

35. 🍞 Institution Narrative — Bread
    - Jesus takes bread
    - Gives thanks
    - Breaks and gives to disciples
    - This is my body

36. 🍷 Institution Narrative — Wine
    - Jesus takes the cup
    - Blood of the covenant
    - Forgiveness of sins
    - Do this in memory of me

37. 🔔 Elevation
    - Consecrated Host raised
    - Chalice raised
    - Bells traditionally rung

38. ✝️ Mystery of Faith
    - Christ has died
    - Christ is risen
    - Christ will come again

39. ❤️ Anamnesis — Remembrance
    - Passion
    - Resurrection
    - Ascension
    - Hope of Christ's return

40. 🩸 Offering of Sacrifice
    - Christ's perfect self-offering
    - Eucharistic sacrifice
    - Accept the offering
    - Reconciliation with God

41. ⛪ Intercessions
    - Pope and bishop
    - Entire Church
    - Living and deceased
    - Mary, apostles, martyrs, saints

42. 🌍 Final Doxology
    - Through him, with him, in him
    - Glory and honor to the Father
    - Unity of the Holy Spirit
    - Great Amen

## 🤝 VII. Communion Rite

43. 🙏 The Lord's Prayer
    - Our Father, who art in heaven
    - Hallowed be thy name
    - Thy kingdom come
    - Thy will be done
    - Give us this day our daily bread
    - Forgive us our trespasses
    - Lead us not into temptation
    - Deliver us from evil

44. 🕊️ Deliverance Prayer
    - Deliver us from evil
    - Peace in our days
    - Hope for Christ's return

45. 👑 Doxology
    - For the kingdom
    - The power
    - And the glory are yours
    - Now and forever

46. ❤️ Prayer for Peace
    - Peace of Christ
    - Unity of the Church

47. 🤝 Sign of Peace
    - Peace of the Lord be with you always
    - And also with you
    - Exchange of peace

48. 🍞 Breaking of Bread
    - Priest breaks the Host
    - Places a piece in the chalice

49. 🐑 Lamb of God
    - Lamb of God, have mercy on us
    - Lamb of God, have mercy on us
    - Lamb of God, grant us peace

50. 🕯️ Invitation to Communion
    - This is the Lamb of God
    - Christ takes away the sins of the world

51. 🙏 Response of Humility
    - Lord, I am not worthy to receive you
    - Prayer for healing

52. 🍷 Holy Communion
    - The body of Christ
    - Amen
    - Reception of Eucharist

53. 🎵 Communion Hymn
    - Singing
    - Thanksgiving
    - Reflection

54. ❤️ Silent Prayer
    - Personal prayer
    - Gratitude
    - Christ's presence

55. 🕊️ Prayer after Communion
    - Thanksgiving
    - Spiritual renewal
    - Amen

## 🌅 VIII. Concluding Rites

56. 📣 Announcements
    - Parish activities
    - Community needs
    - Upcoming celebrations

57. ✝️ Final Blessing
    - The Lord be with you
    - And also with you
    - Father, Son, Holy Spirit
    - Amen

58. 🌎 Dismissal
    - Go in peace to love and serve the Lord
    - Thanks be to God

59. 🎶 Recessional Hymn
    - Priest and servers depart
    - Congregation sings

60. ❤️ Living the Mass
    - Love God
    - Love your neighbor
    - Forgive others
    - Serve those in need
    - Carry Christ's peace into the world

---

## ✨ IX. Three Mysteries — Past, Present, Future

61. ✝️ Sacrifice — PAST
    - He suffered
    - He died
    - He was buried
    - Love through self-sacrifice

62. 🍞 Communion — PRESENT
    - Do this in memory of me
    - Bread and wine
    - Body and blood of Christ
    - Unity and reconciliation

63. 🕊️ Resurrection — FUTURE
    - Christ is risen
    - Christ will come again
    - Resurrection of the dead
    - Life everlasting

---

## ❤️ X. Three Lessons of the Mass

64. 🙏 FAITH
    - Believe
    - Remember
    - Hope

65. ❤️ LOVE
    - Compassion
    - Forgiveness
    - Sacrifice

66. 🌍 SERVICE
    - Care for others
    - Build community
    - Go in peace

---

## ♾️ XI. The Continuous Cycle

67. 🌅 LIFE
    - Birth
    - Learning
    - Love

68. ✝️ DEATH
    - Suffering
    - Sacrifice
    - Remembrance

69. 🕊️ RESURRECTION
    - Renewal
    - Reconciliation
    - Eternal life

## ⛪ THE MASS — ONE SENTENCE

Remember Christ's life, death, and resurrection;
receive his love through Communion;
go forth to love and serve others.
