# 🕊️ In Loving Memory of Cecelia “Ceil” Wacker

## 🌹 December 16, 1950 – September 19, 2015
### ❤️ Age 64 · Wife · Mother · Grandmother · Sister · Friend

---

## 🌸 1. A Life of Love, Grace, and Courage

Cecelia “Ceil” Wacker was born on December 16, 1950,
to Edward and Elizabeth Rebholz.

The youngest of eight children, Ceil grew up surrounded
by a large family whose relationships would remain
an important part of her life.

On June 18, 1970, Ceil married Terrance “Terry” Wacker.

Together they raised four children:

- 👨 Jason Wacker
- 👨 Aaron Wacker
- 👨 Nathan Wacker
- 👩 Nicole Wacker

Ceil's life was defined by her devotion to family,
her sense of humor, her selflessness, and her determination.

She brought warmth and love to those around her and
demonstrated remarkable courage through her battle
with cancer.

She passed away on September 19, 2015, at age 64.

Her legacy lives on through her husband, children,
grandchildren, siblings, extended family, and everyone
whose life she touched.
